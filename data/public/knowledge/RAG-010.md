### RAG-010: Weather-Based Predictive Multipliers
Environmental factors drastically alter consumption patterns. The AI must cross-reference upcoming weather forecasts with the baseline inventory thresholds.

*   **Heatwave (שרב) / Over 32°C:** 
    *   Increase baseline Draught Beer orders by 1.4x.
    *   Increase Aperol and White Wine / Prosecco baseline by 1.5x.
    *   Decrease Heavy Whiskey and Red Wine orders by 0.7x.
    *   *Ice Alert:* Ice machine depletion rate doubles. AI must alert management to order backup commercial ice bags.
*   **Heavy Rain / Winter Storm:**
    *   Decrease Outside Bar inventory baselines by 0.5x (or assume closure of the outside section if severe).
    *   Increase Red Wine and Whiskey baselines by 1.2x for the Inside Bar.
*   **Mild / Spring Weather (20-25°C):** Apply standard 1.0x baseline multipliers.
