### RAG-011: Staff Hierarchy & Authorization Levels
The AI must identify the sender's role to determine if an action or report is authorized.

*   **Ahmesh (אחמ"ש - Shift Manager):** High Authority. Can authorize VIP Comps exceeding the standard limit, approve voids, and override par levels. Names currently holding this role: Roei (רועי), Yael (יעל).
*   **Bartender (ברמן/ברמנית):** Standard Authority. Can log inventory, report spillage, and request backups. Cannot authorize voids or large comps. Names: Shir (שיר), Tomer (תומר), Maya (מאיה), Muriel (מוריאל).
*   **Barback (ברבק):** Support Authority. Handles physical movement of inventory. Their WhatsApp reports usually involve moving stock from storage to the bar (e.g., "העליתי ארגז רדבול"). Names: Ido (עידו).
*   *Security/Bouncer (מאבטח):* Not involved in inventory, but may report ejections which disrupt service.
