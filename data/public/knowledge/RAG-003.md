### RAG-003: Supplier Directory & Logistics
Authorized distributor profiles, contact information, and strict procurement parameters.

**1. Hakerem Distillers**
*   **Brands:** Jameson, Campari, Absolut Vodka, Hendrick's Gin
*   **Delivery Days:** Monday and Thursday
*   **Minimum Order:** Full cases only (12 bottles per case).

**2. IBBL (Israel Beer Breweries Ltd)**
*   **Brands:** Carlsberg, Tuborg, Malka (Distribution), Weihenstephan
*   **Delivery Days:** Tuesday and Friday
*   **Minimum Order:** 3 kegs (mixed 30L/50L allowed) or 5 cases of bottled beer.

**3. CBC (Central Bottling Company)**
*   **Brands:** Coca-Cola, Sprite, Tonic Water, Soda Water, Red Bull
*   **Delivery Days:** Wednesday and Sunday
*   **Minimum Order:** 10 cases (mixed flats allowed).
