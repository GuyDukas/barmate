### RAG-001: Alcohol Volume Conversion Rules
This section defines the standard volumetric measurements for all inventory calculations.

*   **Standard Spirit Bottle:** 700ml
*   **House Pouring Brands (Well Drinks):** 1000ml (1L)
*   **Standard Wine Bottle:** 750ml
*   **Standard Shot:** 60ml
*   **Standard Chaser:** 30ml
*   **Standard Beer Keg:** 30,000ml (30L) - Yields approximately 90 x 330ml servings.
*   **Large Beer Keg:** 50,000ml (50L) - Yields approximately 150 x 330ml servings.
