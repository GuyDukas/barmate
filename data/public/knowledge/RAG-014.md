### RAG-014: Maintenance & Emergency Protocols
System-level understanding of physical bar infrastructure failures.

*   **"המקרר נפל" / Fridge Failure:** If reported, all perishable garnishes and dairy (if applicable) must be marked as 100% loss within 4 hours. White wine and beer sales will halt.
*   **"אין מים" / Water Cut:** Dishwasher cannot operate. Glassware will run out in approximately 45 minutes during a rush.
*   **"הקופה קרסה" / POS Crash:** Staff will switch to manual ticketing. AI must expect a massive data gap in digital sales and rely 100% on WhatsApp text reports for inventory depletion during the crash window.
