### RAG-012: Consumables & Non-Alcoholic Inventory Tracking
The bar cannot operate without dry goods. AI must monitor these based on implicit depletion rates correlated with drink sales.

*   **Napkins (מפיות):** 1 unit depleted per 2 drinks sold. Box contains 500 units. Reorder threshold: 3 boxes.
*   **Straws (קשים):** 1 unit depleted per 1 Long Drink or Cocktail sold (Excludes beer and neat spirits). Box contains 250 units. Reorder threshold: 4 boxes.
*   **Coasters (תחתיות):** 1 unit depleted per Draught Beer sold. Supplied free by IBBL. AI should automatically request more from IBBL rep when beer orders exceed 10 kegs in a week.
*   **CO2 Gas Cylinders (בלוני גז):** Crucial for Draught Beer. 1 standard CO2 cylinder pushes exactly 10 x 50L kegs. AI must track total kegs emptied. Once 8 kegs are depleted since the last CO2 change, AI must alert: "Warning: CO2 replacement needed soon."
