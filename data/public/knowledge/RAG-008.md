### RAG-008: Expanded Bartender Jargon (Part 2)
Further mapping of Hebrew and English nightlife operational slang.

*   **'ברח' / 'Walk-out':** A table left without paying. The physical inventory is depleted, but POS shows an open, unpaid ticket. Must be logged under "Shrinkage - Theft".
*   **'בוטל' / 'Voided':** A drink was poured but returned/refused. Inventory is depleted, POS shows 0 ILS revenue. Must be logged as "Shrinkage - Void".
*   **'פיק' / 'Peak' or 'ראשי / Rush:** Maximum capacity. Expect a 15-20% drop in reporting accuracy during this period. AI should rely heavily on POS data rather than WhatsApp texts during timestamps marked as "Rush".
*   **'פתוח' / 'Open':** An active bottle currently fitted with a pour spout on the speed rack.
*   **'סגור' / 'Closed':** A sealed bottle in reserve.
*   **'שטיחים' / 'Mats':** Rubber bar mats. (Non-inventory context: usually mentioned during opening/closing duties).
*   **'חתול' / 'Cat':** Slang for a specific cleaning task or dealing with spills under the bar. Does not affect alcohol inventory.
*   **'גיבוי' / 'Gibuy':** Same as 'Backup'. Requesting stock from the main storage room.
*   **'דופק' / 'Dofek':** Running smoothly or selling fast (e.g., "הקרלסברג דופק" = Carlsberg is selling rapidly).
