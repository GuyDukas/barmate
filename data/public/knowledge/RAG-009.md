### RAG-009: Supplier RMA (Return Merchandise Authorization) & Credit
Protocols for handling damaged goods, flat kegs, and supplier discrepancies.

*   **Flat Beer Kegs (IBBL):** If a bartender logs "חבית מקציפה" (keg is excessively foaming) or "חבית פגומה" (defective keg) and replaces it before it is empty, the AI must NOT deduct a full 30L/50L as sales. It must log the item as "RMA - Supplier Credit" and draft an alert for the manager to return the keg to IBBL on Tuesday/Friday for a refund.
*   **Broken Delivery (Hakerem):** If a bottle is broken upon delivery (before entering the bar's active stock), it must be logged as "Supplier Breakage". Hakerem requires photographic evidence within 24 hours. The AI should remind the manager to take a photo if "broken delivery" is detected in the chat.
*   **Missing Items from Invoice:** If the delivery invoice says 10 cases of Coca-Cola but the staff logs "קיבלנו רק 9 ארגזי קולה", the AI must flag a discrepancy of -1 case and prepare a credit request text for CBC.
