### RAG-002: Bartender Jargon & Slang Mapping
Dictionary for parsing informal shift reports, WhatsApp logs, and staff text into exact volumetric data.

**Volume Indicators:**
*   **'almost dead'** / **'dead'** / **'אצבע'** = 50ml remaining
*   **'quarter'** / **'רבע'** = 175ml remaining
*   **'half bottle'** / **'חצי בקבוק'** = 350ml remaining
*   **'three quarters'** / **'שלושת רבעי'** = 525ml remaining
*   **'dry'** / **'נגמר'** / **'מת'** = 0ml remaining

**Industry Slang & Operational Terms:**
*   **'ספייר' / 'backup'** = 1 full unopened bottle in the storage room (700ml or 1000ml, depending on brand).
*   **'הרים קנה' / 'harim kaneh'** = Draught line is empty; keg is at 0L and requires replacement.
*   **'מזיע' / 'sweating'** = Keg is running dangerously low, roughly 10% full (approx. 3L left).
*   **'גארניש נקי' / 'clean garnish'** = Citrus (lemon/lime) supply is fully depleted.
*   **'בלי אבנים' / 'no stones'** = Ice machine is empty; triggers an emergency ice delivery protocol.
*   **'פצצה' / 'bomb'** = A sudden, massive rush of orders at a specific station, potentially explaining untracked spillage.
*   **'נפל' / 'dropped'** = Bottle breakage; inventory is at 0ml, must be logged as shrinkage, not sales.
