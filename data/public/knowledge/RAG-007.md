### RAG-007: Station Par Levels & Location Mapping
The venue is divided into two operational zones: "Inside Bar" (בר בפנים) and "Outside Bar" (בר בחוץ). If a bartender reports a shortage without specifying the location, the AI must infer the location based on the exclusive inventory mapped below.

**Inside Bar (Main Cocktail & Premium Station):**
*   **Exclusive Stock:** Patron Silver, Sweet Vermouth, Espresso Machine (all coffee-related reports come from here), Premium Whiskeys (Glenfiddich).
*   **Draught Beer:** Carlsberg (50L), Tuborg (50L).
*   **Par Level Requirement:** Minimum 2 bottles of all pouring spirits at the start of shift.

**Outside Bar (High-Volume & Beer Station):**
*   **Exclusive Stock:** Malka Beer (Draught 30L), Weihenstephan (Draught 30L), Aperol (High volume par level), Slushie Machine (מכונת ברד).
*   **Draught Beer:** Carlsberg (30L), Malka (30L), Weihenstephan (30L).
*   **Par Level Requirement:** Focused on speed. Minimum 4 backup kegs in the adjacent outdoor cooler.
*   *Inference Rule:* If a message says "Malka is dead" or "Slushie machine empty", it implicitly originates from the Outside Bar.
