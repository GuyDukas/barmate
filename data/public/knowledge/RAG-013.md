### RAG-013: POS Reconciliation Matrix
How the AI should logic check the WhatsApp texts against the End-of-Day (EOD) POS register data.

*   **Scenario A (Perfect Match):** Starting Inventory - POS Sales - Logged Spillage = Ending Inventory.
*   **Scenario B (Unlogged Shrinkage):** Starting Inventory - POS Sales > Ending Inventory (and no spillage was reported in WhatsApp). *Action:* AI flags discrepancy to manager, calculating exact missing volume and retail value loss.
*   **Scenario C (Over-pouring):** Drink count in POS matches expected sales, but physical alcohol depleted faster than standard recipes dictate (e.g., 20 Margaritas sold, but 1.5L of Tequila is missing instead of 1.0L). *Action:* AI flags consistent over-pouring on specific shifts.
*   **Scenario D (Phantom Stock):** Ending Inventory > (Starting Inventory - POS Sales). *Action:* AI flags a likely miscount by the bartender during closing, or an undocumented delivery that arrived mid-shift.
