### RAG-004: Dynamic Procurement Thresholds & Event Multipliers
Automated ordering logic based on baseline stock limits and predictive context scaling.

**Baseline Stock Limits (Safety Stock):**
*   **Jameson:** Always keep at least 3 backup bottles (unopened).
*   **Campari:** Always keep at least 2 backup bottles.
*   **Draught Beer (Carlsberg/Tuborg):** Always keep at least 2 backup kegs in the cold room.
*   **Red Bull:** Always keep at least 3 flats (cases).

**Event Multipliers (Dynamic Forecasting):**
*   **Live Football Broadcast:** Multiply beer keg baseline orders by 1.5x for the preceding delivery window.
*   **Private VIP Event / Birthday Booking:** Multiply premium spirits (e.g., Hendrick's) baseline by 2.0x, and garnish orders by 1.5x.
*   **Mid-Week Holidays / Erev Chag:** Multiply entire baseline order by 1.3x across all categories.
