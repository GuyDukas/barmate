### RAG-006: Core Cocktail Recipes & Complex Depletion Metrics
When a specific cocktail is rung up in the POS system, the AI must deduct the exact volumes of multiple ingredients from the digital inventory. Standard garnish is 1 unit unless otherwise noted.

*   **Aperol Spritz:** 90ml Prosecco, 60ml Aperol, 30ml Soda Water, Orange slice garnish. (Note: Prosecco depletion must be tracked per 750ml bottle).
*   **Margarita:** 50ml Patron Silver Tequila, 20ml Triple Sec, 30ml Fresh Lime Juice.
*   **Negroni:** 30ml Campari, 30ml Hendrick's Gin, 30ml Sweet Vermouth.
*   **Mojito:** 50ml White Rum (Bacardi), 25ml Fresh Lime Juice, 15ml Simple Syrup, 8-10 Mint leaves, Top with Soda Water.
*   **Espresso Martini:** 40ml Absolut Vodka, 20ml Coffee Liqueur (Kahlua), 30ml Fresh Espresso, 10ml Simple Syrup.
*   **Gin & Tonic (House):** 50ml House Gin, 150ml Tonic Water (half a standard 330ml can/bottle), Lemon twist.
*   **Long Island Iced Tea:** 15ml Vodka, 15ml Rum, 15ml Gin, 15ml Tequila, 15ml Triple Sec, 25ml Lemon Juice, Top with 50ml Coca-Cola. (High depletion risk alert: deducts from 5 different spirit bottles simultaneously).
