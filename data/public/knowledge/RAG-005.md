### RAG-005: Spillage, Happy Hour, and VIP Protocols
Rules for reconciling missing physical inventory against unstructured text updates and POS discrepancies.

*   **Spillage & Breakage (פחת):** Staff will text terms like "broken," "dropped," or "spilled" (e.g., "spilled Campari"). The system must immediately deduct the corresponding volume (700ml for a full drop, or the estimated remainder) from the physical count and classify it as operational loss. This accounts for physical depletion with zero corresponding POS revenue.
*   **VIP Comps (פינוקים):** Logged in text as "on the house," "VIP table," or "צ'ייסרים עליי". The system should anticipate a 0 ILS transaction in the POS system linked to this specific inventory deduction. Standard VIP comp threshold is 10 chasers (300ml) or 1 full bottle (700ml). Deductions exceeding this limit require manager approval.
*   **Happy Hour Protocol (1+1):** Active daily between 18:00 and 20:30. During this window, POS sales ring up at 1x revenue, but physical depletion is 2x. If the timestamp of a POS transaction falls within this window, the system must double the deducted volume for draught beers and house spirits to accurately align digital stock with physical reality.
